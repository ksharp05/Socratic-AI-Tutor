# Spike S4 · Replacing Llama Guard 4 as the content-safety check

**Owner:** R4 · Swati · **Date:** 9 Nov 2026 · **Script:** [`scripts/r4/guard_model_spike.py`](../../scripts/r4/guard_model_spike.py)

## Question

`meta-llama/llama-guard-4-12b` is not on the course key's model list (`course_models` in `config/app.toml`), and no other dedicated guard model is either. Which allowed model can act as the content-safety classifier?

## Method

- Three candidates: `meta-llama/llama-3.1-8b-instruct` (same family; Llama Guard 3's base model), `anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5` and `amazon/nova-lite-v1` (different families).
- One six-slot classifier prompt, temperature 0, JSON output `{"label": SAFE|UNSAFE, "category": ...}`.
- 10 student-style messages: 5 safe maths questions (2 of them deliberately tricky, e.g. "This quadratic is killing me"), and 5 mildly unsafe (distress, violence, sexual, hate, alcohol).
- One call per model per message, run once from my laptop with my own course key.

## Results

| Model | Right | False blocks | Missed | Errors | Median latency | Cost (10 calls) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `meta-llama/llama-3.1-8b-instruct` | **10/10** | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1812 ms | $0.00005 |
| `anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5` | **10/10** | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1116 ms | $0.00050 |
| `amazon/nova-lite-v1` | 9/10 | 0 | 0 | 1 (unparsed reply on the sexual-content case) | 929 ms | $0.00017 |

## Recommendation

Use **`meta-llama/llama-3.1-8b-instruct`**: tied best accuracy, about 10× cheaper than Haiku, and it follows the plan's "closest model from the same family" rule. Backup: `anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5` if latency matters more.

**Fail closed:** if the classifier errors or its reply cannot be parsed, treat the message as UNSAFE.

## Limits

- 10 hand-written development examples, one run: a sanity check, not evidence of robustness. The real measure is E2 (R5's attack suite, which R4 has not seen).
- The unsafe examples are mild and in English; indirect or Hinglish phrasing is untested.
- Adds about 1.8 s per check. Wiring it into `guards.py` needs the Lead's go-ahead because behaviour was frozen on 6 Nov.
