# v1.0 — course submission, 12 November 2026

A Socratic maths tutor for Class 10 students, covering NCERT chapters 4 (Quadratic Equations), 5 (Arithmetic Progressions) and 8 (Introduction to Trigonometry). It gives hints as questions, checks answers in code, cites its sources and never hands over the answer.

- **Team tutor.**
  - One LangGraph session graph with input and output guards.
  - Four FastMCP tool servers, behind role-checked tool hooks.
  - A teacher approval gate.
  - A Streamlit UI on a FastAPI service.
  - `docker compose up --build --wait` starts it. The `ops` profile adds Prometheus, Grafana and the answer-leak alert.
- **Live beta.** https://socratic-tutor-beta.vercel.app/beta/, on Vercel with Neon Postgres. It has:
  - consent before any model call;
  - saved progress per browser profile;
  - forget-my-data;
  - crisis, abuse and secrecy screens in English, Hindi and Hinglish.

  Model replies need a funded provider key. Without one, the site answers with labelled, cited source reading.

The release check passed: health; consent required; 2/2 live replies with citations; teacher claim and crisis message handled without the model; practice and grading; quiz isolation; consent withdrawal; forget-my-data.

- **Independent testing.** A public test set built from 15 free, human-labelled datasets ([details](../testing/PUBLIC_TESTSET.md)). On its 4,000-message held-out core, the D-7 classifier (Gemma 4 31B, confirmed by the Lead on 12 Nov) caught 68/88 crisis messages, where today's screens caught 27/88.
- **Known limits.**
  - Simulated students only.
  - No teacher or human review is recorded.
  - The safety classifier (D-7) is confirmed but stays switched off: it wrongly blocks about 18% of safe messages.
  - The live site's provider key is a course key valid until 16 Nov 2026. After that, replies fall back to cited source reading.
  - The beta's mobile check is pending.
  - The saved-question queue switches on only after the schema-2 migration.
