# Data sufficiency audit

The saved data is sufficient for bounded engineering regression and a reproducibility check. It is **not sufficient for a final effectiveness claim**. More paid repetitions of the same synthetic cards would not change that.

| Data | Available | What it can support |
|---|---:|---|
| Public practice bank | 665 items | Public mathematical task inventory; not 665 independently reviewed lessons |
| Fixed comparison | 32 shared journeys, 42 turns/version | Before/after functionality on public developer cases |
| Formatting/control regressions | 122 checks | Whitespace invariance, preserved numeric layout and control precedence |
| C1/C2 baseline | 16 shared development cards × 3 seeds per arm | Reproducibility and descriptive development metrics |
| Security replay | 80 shared cases per guard condition | 53 supported cases/condition; unknowns retained |
| New concept help | 12 explanations, 7 reference cards | Specific direct-answer coverage; human review pending |
| New human labels | 0 | No human validity or teaching-quality conclusion yet |

Eligibility does not publish a question: actual teacher review and version-bound approval are still required. The 12 quiz items are AI-authored development checks, not independent final assessment. AP's five additional exact adapters are unit-tested but remain outside the connected pool; AP recognition and other chapter families also require parser/checker/flow qualification before connection. More item count cannot substitute for that work.

## What must be added before final claims

1. Actual R3/R2 scope and architecture review, settling which of the 26 provisional misconception types are supported and what evidence distinguishes an error from a correct statement or an ordinary question.
2. Independently authored assessment and learner cases across that accepted scope, including matched correct controls, unfamiliar phrasings, ambiguous and self-corrected work, and numeric/context variants. Keep authorship and development/final separation explicit before freeze. The assistant-authored 24-card/30-pair candidate is not independent final data.
3. Actual teacher review of references, teaching replies and lesson/question sets. Two independent raters must complete and reconcile labels; R1/R5 are the same person and do not supply two raters.
4. A preregistered final protocol that separates engineering outcomes, synthetic proxy scores and any learner outcomes. Choose final sample sizes for the accepted effect and design; the current 32-case convenience panel supplies no power-based learning claim.
5. Broader measured security evidence for the 27 unknown cases/condition, independent installation and actual remote CI evidence. Application overlays do not prove resistance to a poisoned on-disk index.

All added records must state who authored/reviewed them. The new 32-pair and 12-source forms are ready but blank. Original frozen source/data and original E1–E4 results retain their original tags; no new study result is inferred from this product work.
