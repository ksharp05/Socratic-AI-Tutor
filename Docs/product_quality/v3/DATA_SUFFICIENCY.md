# Fresh-data sufficiency and remaining gates

The 360 messages cover 30 public types in three chapters: 12/type, with four proposed incorrect claims and eight correct/question/correction/insufficient-evidence controls. The 120/120/120 partitions were fixed before testing; both builds were archived before inspection or tuning. No cases were used to change R3 diagnosis code.

This is enough to expose a broad current detection gap and false-label risks. It is **not enough for final release or learning claims**:

- Only four proposed positive paraphrases/type; type-level rates are unstable. Label disagreement, ambiguity and invalid quoted evidence remain explicit.
- One generated batch/type creates correlation. Scenario partitions differ deliberately; they are not random exchangeable held-out samples. Explicit Hinglish slots occur only in development, so cross-partition results cannot establish multilingual generalization.
- A separate AI family reviewed blinded messages, but two-model agreement cannot replace teacher labels or independent raters.
- Fresh-state, single-turn stub sessions do not test live conversational quality, long-term learning, realistic learner behavior or deployment. No new C1/C2 comparison was run; existing matched results and limits remain unchanged.
- Citation checks establish identity, hashes and chapter matching only. Meaning, age-appropriate teaching and safety need human review. CI security smokes are not comprehensive; unsupported original E2 channels remain unsupported.

Next: R3/R2 triage private failures and label disagreements against the public catalogue; actual teachers review case truth, false-positive controls and worked examples; independently author and lock final cases before candidate freeze; preregister metrics and sample size for the intended scope; run fresh matched C1/C2 and supported-channel safety checks after runtime/content acceptance. Do not spend the budget repeating weak studies or count disputed labels as successes.

Original frozen studies, data, blank teacher packets and reports remain intact. R1 and R5 being one person does not create two independent raters. Formal M3/M4 acceptance remains open.
