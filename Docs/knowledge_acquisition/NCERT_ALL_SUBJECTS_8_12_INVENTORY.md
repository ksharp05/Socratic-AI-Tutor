## Publisher exceptions and repairs

1. **Class 9 ICT:** the active NCERT menu says Coming Soon and provides no active textbook download.
2. **Class 11 Urdu, Gulistan-e-Adab (`kuga1`):** the full-book ZIP contains only six PDF members, including two empty files and a truncated chapter. All 22 chapter URLs and prelims were downloaded individually. Twenty-one chapters and prelims validate. Chapter 04 remains malformed at NCERT and fails both pypdf and Poppler; it is explicitly excluded from readable links. The defective original archive and the direct-download receipts are preserved.
3. **Class 12 Urdu, Gulistan-e-Adab (`luga1`):** all 11 numbered archive PDFs are retained; the catalogue parameter says 12 but the nominal chapter-12 URL returns HTTP 404. The 2019-20 front matter and retained content page extents disagree. Full original-edition coverage is unverified.
4. **Catalogue numbering:** extra dots in filenames are preserved and recognized; literary sections use separate numeric ranges; current Hornbill/Antra navigation differs from stale dropdown counts. `COVERAGE_RESOLUTIONS.json` records each finding. The common Urdu grammar extra nominal indices 23/24 return 404, while retained page extents agree with its contents.
5. **Science parser exceptions:** one Class 10 Science PDF and two Class 12 Biology PDFs exceed pypdf’s 75 MB decompression bound. The bound was retained; an independent Poppler parser and all 46 page renders passed (6, 25 and 15 pages). These files require the recorded alternate extraction route.

No corrupt asset was relabelled valid, no missing chapter was generated, and no publisher archive was overwritten. The inventory reports observed editions/reprints where established; not every archive is a 2026–27 edition.

- All 145 archive hashes match their acquisition records, and all downloaded ZIPs passed member CRC checks. Eight Maths archives and reading copies were reused unchanged; 137 additional archives were downloaded.

- Page boxes and content streams were checked with pypdf, with the explicit independent renderer exception above. Twenty representative title pages were rendered and visually inspected by the assistant across grades, sciences, other subjects and languages; this is not human content approval or all-page visual review.
- Duplicate source hashes are indexed without deleting publisher originals. All previous Maths reading copies and the earlier nine-source Class 10 pool retain their hashes. No source text was included in the shared metadata inventory.
- The local library has 145 book cards; every relative file link was checked to exist. The damaged chapter has no readable link. No tutor runtime or teaching scope was changed.
