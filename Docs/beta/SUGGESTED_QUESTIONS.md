# Questions that work well in the live beta

These questions all got a full live reply in the 100-question check on 6 November 2026 (https://socratic-tutor-beta.vercel.app/beta/). Each reply explained one idea, cited a source and ended with a focused question. Each question was asked once, in a new conversation, by the coding assistant. Treat the list as a demo guide, not a teacher-reviewed evaluation.

The live beta covers three Class 10 Mathematics chapters: Quadratic Equations, Arithmetic Progressions and Introduction to Trigonometry.

## Quadratic Equations

- Why can't the coefficient of x² be zero in a quadratic?
- What is the discriminant used for?
- If the discriminant is negative, what does that say about the roots?
- When should I use the quadratic formula instead of factorising?
- Can a quadratic equation have exactly one real root?
- Why does the quadratic formula have a ± sign?
- Can the roots of a quadratic be fractions?
- A rectangle's length is 4 m more than its breadth and its area is 96 m². What equation describes it?

## Arithmetic Progressions

- How can I tell whether a list of numbers forms an AP?
- Is 1, 4, 9, 16, … an arithmetic progression?
- Is the sequence of odd numbers an AP?
- Can an AP have all its terms equal?
- What is the formula for the sum of the first n terms of an AP?
- Why does the sum formula use n/2?
- Why is the nth term a + (n − 1)d and not a + nd?
- How do I find the common difference if I only know the 3rd and 7th terms?
- If I save ₹50 more each month than the month before, how is that an AP?
- If the sum of the first n terms is 3n² + 5n, how do I find the AP?
- Are √2, √8, √18, √32 in AP?

## Introduction to Trigonometry

- What does tan mean in a right triangle?
- Why is sin θ never more than 1 for an acute angle?
- What is sec θ?
- How is cot related to tan?
- Why is tan 90° not defined?
- Why is cos 0° equal to 1?
- What happens to cos θ as θ increases from 0° to 90°?
- Does the value of sin θ depend on the size of the triangle?
- Can sin A and cos A ever be equal?
- In triangle ABC right-angled at B, which side is opposite angle C?

## What to avoid for now

These currently get a weak or generic reply. Fixes are planned.

- **An `=` sign, or the words solve, calculate, check, right or correct.** Examples: "sin 30° = 1, right?", "What does l mean in S = n/2 (a + l)?", "Solve x² − 9x + 20 = 0". These get a fixed "What quantities are given, and what is your first step?" reply without the model. Use the **Practice** panel for worked problems.
- **Other subjects or maths chapters**, such as probability, coordinate geometry, Pythagoras, calculus or Science. The hosted beta has only the three chapters above, so these get "I couldn't match that question to a source."
- **"Give me a practice question" or "teach me something new".** These are not routed to the practice feature yet.
- **Textbook exercise numbers**, such as "question 3 of exercise 4.2".
- **Hindi-English questions about trigonometry.** The AP one worked; the trigonometry one did not.
