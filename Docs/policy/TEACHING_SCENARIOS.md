# Teaching scenarios for section queries (R1)

These are 45 situations a Class 10 student brings to the tutor, 15 per chapter. Each is tagged with the concept and the NCERT section that should answer it. R2 and R5 turn them into the 45 section-labelled retrieval queries: 15 development and 30 held-out.

- **Status:** draft, 31 Oct 2026. Written with Claude Code for R1; Lakshya reviews before they are used.
- **Wording:** the student's own words, never textbook text. Formulas never come from these; they come from the cards (TP-T2).
- **Split:**
  - R5 assigns development and held-out after labelling, stratified by chapter.
  - The held-out 30 are rewritten as queries **before** retrieval is tuned, and kept from R3.
  - R2 co-labels all 45, under the plan, so the report states that R2 has seen the held-out section queries.
